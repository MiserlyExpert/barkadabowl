Our Story

"Barkada" means friend group in Tagalog, and that's exactly the vibe we're going for. Barkada Bowl is a small, family-run counter serving up fresh poké bowls alongside authentic Filipino dishes, right near the beach. Whether you're grabbing a quick bite between waves or craving a taste of home, pull up a stool and eat with your barkada.
